  ELCHEMY FACTS supports, such as how quickly samples go out or the documentation that comes
  with an order.

  but don't say anything is stocked there or how fast it ships unless a Fact says so.
- Name one of their chemicals when SIGNALS list products.
- Ask: would samples or a quote help? Invite a short reply, or offer the booking link.
- Follow-up angle: a different point a Fact supports, or a simple offer to send a sample.
